The 50- and 100-word sets keep their original news and online frequency list. The 500-, 1,000-, and 2,000-word sets use a Japanese news frequency ranking, filtered to unique romaji words with a sense-matched example.

Meanings and example sentences come from the TKG Japanese-English Learner’s Dictionary. Its entries include sense-tagged examples and are reviewed as part of the project. TKG data is public domain (CC0).

The larger-set frequency ranking comes from Noos’s Japanese list, based on Leipzig Corpora Collection news counts. The list is CC BY-SA 4.0. The 50- and 100-word lists retain the original source.
